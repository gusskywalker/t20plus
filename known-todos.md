RACES

Kallyanach — "+2 in one attribute OR +1 in two" is a player choice between two allocation shapes, not a fixed distribution.

Golem — stats depend on a chosen chassis material (8 options) plus a separate size choice; no single fixed block.

Kobolds — it's a swarm (multiple creatures acting as one entity), not a normal single-character race.

Duende — fully build-your-own (nature, size, attributes, powers all chosen individually) — there's no fixed baseline to encode.
